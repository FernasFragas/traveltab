# 7a — Pins and the place panel

Source: `tasks/todo.md` 7a; `tasks/plan.md` **Target Output** (pins, labels, panel); `docs/writers-map-contract.md` **Place panel** and **Browser extension API**.

## Journeys

- As a traveler, I want writer pins (orange, with their writer count) to stand out above notable places (blue), and the biggest places labelled, so I can see at a glance where writers went.
- As a traveler, I want to open a place and see, in order: photo, name, *kind · area*, who mentioned it, the AI summary, description, opening hours, Wikipedia and **Save to plan**, so I can decide whether to go.
- As a traveler, I want "View writers' posts (N)" to expand into every post with its blog and language, so I can read the writers on their own sites. No blog text is quoted.
- As a traveler on a base-only destination, I want "No writer mentions here yet" instead of empty boxes.

## Decisions

- **Layer names:** `notable` and `writers`, registered with `TravelMap.registerLayer`. 7f toggles them by these names; 7b uses `plan` and 7c will use `itineraries`.
- **Big places:** the five places with the most Wikimedia sitelinks get a permanent label.
- **Monograms** are drawn in the browser from the writer links' `data-writer-host` and `data-blog-name`: initials of the blog name, a colour hashed from the host, up to three and then "+N". The template has no function map, and the handler is not 7a's file.
- **Panel loading** uses `htmx.ajax` into the `panel` slot, so `htmx:afterSwap` fires and 7d can repaint its Save buttons.
- **"No writer mentions here yet"** shows in the panel of any place with no mentions, which covers every place on a base-only destination.
