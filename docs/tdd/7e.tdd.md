# 7e — Seed saved places into the plan (`Include`): TDD Evidence

**Source task:** `tasks/todo.md` 7e. Files: `internal/planner/types.go`, `internal/planner/build.go` (+ `build_test.go`), `internal/adapters/httpserver/trip.go` (+ `trip_test.go`), `views/itinerary_body.go.tpl` ("Also suggested" block).

## User journeys

- As a traveler, I want the places I saved to be in my plan, so that my days are built around them.
- As a traveler with saves close together, I want them on the same day.
- As a traveler who saved too many, I want the extras listed, not lost.
- As a traveler who saved nothing, or with the writers' map off, I want the same plan as before.
- As a visitor, I want an unknown or repeated saved QID to be ignored, not to break the plan.
